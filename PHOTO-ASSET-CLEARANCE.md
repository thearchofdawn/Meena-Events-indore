# Meena Events — photo asset clearance

## Important

The project currently uses clearly labelled SVG placeholders. Do not replace them with Google Maps customer-uploaded photos by downloading or copying those files: Google Maps listing images may belong to customers or photographers, and appearing on a public listing does not grant website reuse rights.

## What to request from Meena Events

Ask the business owner to provide original high-resolution photographs or written permission to use specific photos from their Google Business Profile / Google Maps listing. Keep the approval message and the photo source with the project records.

Recommended minimum photo set:

1. 1 wide hero photograph (at least 2000 px wide)
2. 3 wedding décor / mandap / stage photographs
3. 2 ceremony or guest-experience photographs
4. 2 corporate or institutional event photographs, if those services are confirmed
5. 2 social celebration photographs
6. 1 team / behind-the-scenes photograph

Before publishing, confirm that the business has permission from the photographer and any identifiable people where required. Avoid publishing private event details, children's faces, or guest close-ups without appropriate permission.

## Where to install approved images

Use the paths already defined in `config/images.ts`. Keep descriptive alt text, use compressed WebP/AVIF where practical, and ensure each image has the correct aspect ratio for its component. Remove the portfolio sample notice only after real project details and photographs have been approved.


## Google Maps integration added

The website now links to the verified Meena Events Indore Google Maps place listing and embeds its map on the Contact page. Visitors can open the listing and view photos there. The website does not copy or host those listing photos. This avoids treating public visibility as a reuse licence.
